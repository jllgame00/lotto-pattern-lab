Goal: test whether causal current gap conditions reveal translation structure hidden in the 2D matrix. Execute inline with tests, independent review, no commits/pushes. The user's Phase7 specification defines the scope.

## Predeclared decisions, before inspecting Phase7 results

- Canonical draws1–1245 only. No source retrieval, target1246 inspection or evaluation. Preserve every existing Phase5/6 artifact byte-for-byte. Separate Phase7 directory/registry.
- Prediction state t uses only draws<t. Gap=t-1-last_appearance_draw. Last100, predicting104 gives3. Never seen is NaN and excluded from state-conditioned rules. Mean historical gap requires at least two previous appearances; relative gap is otherwise missing.
- Sparse table: target_draw, feature_cutoff=t-1, number, pre-draw gap, historical mean gap, relative gap, appeared_next (main-number label for target t). No dense cube. Winning points use their PRE-draw states.
- Encoder fits finite pre-draw states in training only: clip raw gap at min(26,ceil(training95th percentile)); exact/clipped states0..cap. The26-draw hard ceiling bounds the search to half-year-scale gaps even in unusual samples. Coarse bins use training1/3 and2/3 quantiles (higher observation convention), labels low/medium/high. Relative bins use fixed boundaries0.5 and1.5, with a causal historical mean. Missing values remain state-1, never assigned to a bin. No threshold search.
- Conditional translation search: dt1..52,dn±1..15, all three representations. Minimum100 observed source opportunities after boundaries. Wilson intervals and raw z are descriptive; complete-search null maxima supply inference.
- Destination analysis: crossed source/destination coarse bins over the same shifts. DESCRIPTIVE ONLY relative to the historical source time; these tables never enter predictors. Minimum100 opportunities.
- True3D search: dt1..13,dn±1..6, delta of clipped PRE-draw gaps -3..3. Minimum100 opportunities. Counts conditional on observed source and realized destination state; no predictive use of this descriptive destination condition.
- Trajectories over dt1..13,dn±1..6: same coarse gap bin (3 rules) or repeated clipped-gap displacement -3..3 (7 rules). Count lengths3/4/5 and report maximal runs only. Continuation statistic conditions on all earlier observed points and final pre-draw state, minimum20 opportunities. Separately calibrate longest trajectory without a minimum-support exclusion.
